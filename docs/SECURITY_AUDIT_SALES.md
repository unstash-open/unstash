# LeadHarbor repository review — sales operating system

## Narrow initial customer profile

The first offer is for teams that meet all of these conditions:

- 2–20 engineers and no dedicated application-security reviewer;
- a GitHub-hosted TypeScript or Node.js product;
- an upcoming release, payment/auth/webhook change or recent security-sensitive migration;
- authority to provide repository access and approve an offline source review;
- willingness to fix and retest confirmed findings.

Do not sell the initial fixed-price offer to regulated enterprises, multi-repository
platforms, production penetration-test buyers, unverified third-party targets or
customers requiring 24/7 incident response. Qualify those separately.

## Qualification questions

1. Which immutable commit and deployment model should be reviewed?
2. What changed recently, and what release decision is waiting on the review?
3. Who owns the repository and can authorize the exact techniques?
4. Can the project build locally with synthetic data?
5. Who will own remediation and attend the report review?

## Outreach cadence

- Day 0: one project-specific observation and a link to the sample report.
- Day 3: one short follow-up tied to a release, auth, payment or webhook trigger.
- Day 7: offer the $490 fixed-scope beta review and its three-business-day target.
- Day 14: close the loop politely; do not continue unsolicited follow-ups.

Never claim a vulnerability before authorization, mass-send identical copy, imply a
guaranteed bounty or use private repository information in outreach.

## Pipeline stages

`researched → contacted → replied → qualified → scope-confirmed → checkout-started → paid → delivered → retest → managed`

Every record needs an owner, next action and next-action date. A stage without a
next action is not an active opportunity.

Record actual delivery time. After three paid beta reviews, reprice or narrow scope
if the median exceeds six hours. Do not sell the managed plan until the release and
pull-request review process has been operated manually for at least one customer.

## Weekly scorecard

- qualified leads added;
- personalized first contacts;
- replies and positive-reply rate;
- qualification calls;
- checkout starts and paid orders;
- delivery hours and on-time rate;
- confirmed findings accepted by maintainers;
- retests completed;
- sample-report views to intake starts;
- managed-plan conversions.

The first commercial milestone is one paid beta review delivered on time. The next
milestone is three paid reviews with measured delivery hours and one publishable,
customer-approved case study.
