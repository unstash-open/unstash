## Nature of contributions

Contributions are voluntary support for an independently operated open-source
software project. They are not purchases, investments, loans or charitable
donations. Contributors receive no equity, token, governance right, repayment,
profit, financial return or tax deduction.

Blockchain transfers are normally irreversible. Contributions cannot be
automatically refunded.

## Accounting rule

The campaign total includes confirmed incoming USDT transfers to either public
campaign wallet on or after **2026-07-23 14:26:45 UTC**. TRONSCAN is the source
of truth for TRC20 transfers and Etherscan is the source of truth for ERC20
transfers. Transfers of other tokens or on other networks do not count toward

## Shipped before funding

On **2026-07-28**, Unstash released a permission-free importer for Reddit's
official `saved_posts.csv` and `saved_comments.csv` files before receiving any
campaign contribution. Files are parsed locally, duplicate links are skipped
and no Reddit account or token is requested.

On the same date, Unstash released Chromium extension developer preview 0.1.
It captures the active tab using only the temporary `activeTab` permission,
passes the capture through a URL fragment that is not sent in HTTP requests and
stores the item in the existing local browser queue.

The project absorbed that proof-of-concept work. The active milestone budget
below applies to cross-browser hardening and release work that remains.
